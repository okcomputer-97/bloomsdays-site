Description

EJmix Aromatherapy Blend & Diluent is an odorless glycol base for essential oils. Use it to dilute and thin your blends for reed diffusers and oil diffusers. Thick oils such as vetiver, sandalwood, myrrh and benzoin mix in easily and stay liquid. EJmix adds no scent of its own and does not go rancid like plant carrier oils. Ingredients: USP grade propylene glycol, PEG 200, PEG 300, PEG 400. Bottled in the USA. For diffuser use only. Do not ingest.
