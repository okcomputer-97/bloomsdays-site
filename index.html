WHY BLOOMSDAYS

Bloomsdays is inspired by James Joyce’s epic “Ulysses”, a novel set during a single day, June 16, 1904, and the seemingly ordinary yet never-the-less Homerian journey undertaken by the protagonist. We strive to recognize the extraordinary in the mundane, to respect the struggles each of us faces, and to evoke a space of reflection and focus on life and its beautiful mysteries.
